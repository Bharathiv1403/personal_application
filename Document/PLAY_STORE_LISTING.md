### 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Take complete command of your life, career, and finances with AAYON — your private, offline-first personal operating system and AI copilot.

Designed specifically for ambitious developers and professionals, AAYON combines productivity, career acceleration, and smart asset tracking into a unified, fluid experience.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🌟 KEY SUPERPOWERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ 1. EXECUTIVE COMMAND CENTER
• Dynamic daily briefing powered by Google Gemini AI.
• Live weather updates and greeting tailored to your local time.
• Rule-of-3 focus priority manager to conquer your day without cognitive overload.

💼 2. CAREER & ATS PIPELINE
• Complete job application tracker across wishlist, applied, interview, and offer stages.
• In-Hand CTC calculator tailored for Indian tax & PF regimes.
• Built-in recruiter email templates and follow-up reminders.
• Direct interview call launching with calendar sync.

💰 3. SMART WEALTH & VATTI ENGINE
• Tailored for Indian finance: easily track monthly Vatti (interest-based personal loans).
• Real-time part-principal repayment recalculator.
• Gold & Sovereign (Savaran) portfolio vault with live gram valuations.
• Trip Splitwise module for spontaneous weekend getaways.

🏃 4. FIT & HEALTH PULSE
• Real hardware pedometer integration for live step counting.
• Daily water hydration ring and sleep quality logging.
• Automatic calories burned and active minutes estimation.

🤖 5. AAYON AI COPILOT
• Powered by Google Gemini.
• Context-aware executive assistant that knows your active health, career, and financial priorities.
• Text-to-speech voice assistant support for on-the-go briefings.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & ON-DEVICE
All your financial and career data is stored strictly on your device using encrypted local storage. Zero cloud harvesting, zero ads, zero data sharing.

### 5. How to Build the Release AAB for Upload:
Run the following in your project terminal:
```bash
npx eas build --profile production --platform android
```
The resulting `.aab` file can be downloaded and directly dropped into the Google Play Console!
